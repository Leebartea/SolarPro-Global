--------------------------------------------------------------------
1b. WHY THE THEMEFOREST DOWNLOAD SHIPS PLACEHOLDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Envato draws a hard line between the two uses, and the licence needed differs:

  Item preview (the live demo)  -> a COMMERCIAL licence is enough.
  Item download (the ZIP)       -> a REDISTRIBUTION licence is required,
                                   meaning the right to RESELL the asset.

  "In order to include an asset you did not create yourself in your item
   download, you need to have an appropriate redistribution license (the rights
   to resell that asset under the applicable Envato License)."
   — What Assets Can I Use In My Items?, Envato Author Support

The Pexels Licence is permissive and very probably satisfies this. "Very
probably" is not a basis for a licensing warranty on a marketplace where
rejection is expensive, so the ThemeForest archive contains no third-party
photographs at all. `scripts/package-themeforest.js` swaps every photograph for
a generated placeholder of identical filename and dimensions, so the template
still renders correctly and the buyer drops their own photos straight in.

This removes the question rather than answering it, and it is what most
established Site Template authors do.
