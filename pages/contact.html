Absolutely — international projects are a core part of our business. We have completed projects in 15+ countries and have established supply chain partnerships and certified local installation partners in key markets including Ghana, South Africa, Kenya, UAE, and India. For markets where we don't have a local team, we provide full engineering design, equipment supply, and remote commissioning support to a local electrical contractor.
The more information you can provide, the more accurate our initial estimate will be. Helpful information includes: your recent electricity bills (last 3–6 months), your property type and approximate size, your location (GPS coordinates if rural), any existing electrical infrastructure (generator size, distribution board rating), and whether you have specific goals (grid independence, backup power, bill reduction, ESG compliance). Don't worry if you don't have all of this — we'll guide you through what we need.
We accept bank transfers (local and international SWIFT/IBAN), Paystack (Nigerian naira), Flutterwave (multiple African currencies), and Stripe (card payments in USD/EUR/GBP). We also work with solar asset finance providers and can structure lease-to-own arrangements for qualifying commercial clients. Typical payment terms are 50% deposit on contract signing and 50% on commissioning.
