Residential Solar

Power Your Home, Slash Your Bill

Our residential solar solutions are designed for homeowners who want to take control of their energy costs. We perform a detailed site assessment — including roof orientation, shading analysis, and local irradiance data — to size a system that delivers maximum financial return for your specific property and consumption patterns.

Whether you need a simple grid-tied rooftop system to reduce your electricity bill, a hybrid system with battery backup to survive load-shedding and power outages, or a fully off-grid setup for remote living — we engineer the right solution, not just the easiest sale.

  • Grid-tied, hybrid (battery backup), and full off-grid configurations available
  • High-efficiency monocrystalline panels (380W – 550W per module)
  • Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄) batteries for long cycle life and safety
  • Remote monitoring app: track generation, consumption, and battery state in real time
  • 5-year workmanship warranty + 25-year panel performance guarantee (from manufacturer)

Typical use case: 3-bedroom home with a ₦80,000/month electricity bill — a 10 kWp hybrid system typically reduces bills to under ₦6,000/month with a payback period of 4–6 years.

Commercial Solar

Reduce Operational Costs at Scale

For businesses, electricity is one of the largest controllable operating expenses. A well-engineered commercial solar system can reduce your energy expenditure by 50–80%, improve operational resilience, and deliver a compelling return on investment — often achieving payback in 3–5 years at commercial scale.

We work with factories, office complexes, retail centres, hotels, schools, hospitals, and telecom operators to engineer systems that match your load profile, available roof or ground area, and energy procurement strategy. Our projects include export-to-grid setups with net metering arrangements where local regulations permit.

  • System sizes from 20 kWp to multi-MW — rooftop, carport, and ground-mount
  • Load-flow analysis and shading simulation using PVsyst modelling software
  • Grid-connection application and DISCO approval management (where applicable)
  • Enterprise SCADA monitoring with real-time performance alerts and monthly reports
  • ESG reporting support: CO₂ offset documentation for sustainability disclosures

Typical use case: A 250 kWp rooftop system on a manufacturing facility in Nigeria saves approximately ₦28M annually in electricity costs with a payback period of approximately 3.5 years.

Off-Grid Systems

Energy Independence Where the Grid Doesn't Reach

Across Africa and in rural regions globally, grid electricity is either unavailable, unreliable, or prohibitively expensive to connect. Off-grid solar systems — combining solar generation with battery storage and sometimes a backup generator — provide 24/7 clean, reliable power without dependence on a utility grid.

We design off-grid systems for rural households and communities, agricultural operations, telecommunications base stations, remote lodges and camps, water pumping stations, and any application where energy reliability is non-negotiable. Every system is sized based on verified daily load profiles and local irradiance data to ensure autonomy even during extended overcast periods.

  • Battery autonomy sizing: minimum 2 days of reserve capacity for critical loads
  • Hybrid charge controllers: solar primary, generator backup for extended cloudy seasons
  • Community microgrid design with prepaid energy metering for multi-household systems
  • Solar water pump systems: DC pump solutions for borehole and surface water applications
  • Remote GSM/GPRS monitoring for unmanned or remote installations

Maintenance & Support

Protect Your Investment for 25 Years

A solar PV system is a 25-year investment. Neglected panels lose 0.5–1% efficiency per year from soiling, microcracking, and degraded connections — losses that compound significantly over time. Our preventive maintenance programmes ensure your system operates at peak performance year after year, protecting your return on investment.

We offer flexible maintenance contracts for installations we commissioned and for third-party systems. Our technicians are trained to service all major inverter brands including SMA, Huawei, ABB, Fronius, Victron, and Growatt.

  • Bi-annual preventive maintenance visits: panel cleaning, torque checks, thermal imaging
  • Inverter diagnostics and firmware updates with performance benchmarking
  • Battery health assessment: capacity testing, electrolyte check (lead-acid), BMS diagnostics (lithium)
  • Continuous remote monitoring with email/SMS alerts for fault conditions
  • Emergency call-out service: 24-hour response SLA for critical system failures

Our Process

How We Work

A structured, transparent process from your first enquiry to a fully commissioned, monitored solar system — and ongoing support beyond.

1

Site Assessment

We visit your site (or analyse remotely using satellite data) to evaluate roof area, orientation, shading, and your electricity consumption data.

2

System Design

Our engineers produce a detailed system design using PVsyst modelling, a full bill of materials, single-line diagrams, and a bankable financial model.

3

Installation

Certified technicians install the racking system, modules, inverter, batteries, cabling, and protection devices to IEC standards and manufacturer specifications.

4

Commissioning

Full system commissioning including insulation resistance tests, earth continuity checks, I-V curve testing, and grid-connection verification. You receive an IEC 62446 test report.

5

Ongoing Support

Monitoring portal access from day one. Annual or bi-annual maintenance visits. Direct line to our technical support team for the life of the system.
